Goc that long

To hay lam tro, hay cot nha, nhung khong phai luc nao cung nhe long.

To bat dau hut thuoc o cap ba vi nhung van de to gap phai. To khong mong nguoi yeu tuong lai tu ep ban than chap nhan viec do, vi to biet no cung chang tot dep gi.

Lop 11 to bat dau bi dau dau, co le day la mot trong so ly do khien to hut thuoc. Len dai hoc, to trai qua kha nhieu song gio, den gio van chang biet vi sao minh van song. Co le mang to lon, hoac server doi van chua kick.

To khong co gu nguoi yeu cu the. To chi thich nguoi thong minh. Va neu nguoi do chiu duoc tinh cợt nha cua to, kha nang cao la cau chuyen se vui hon nhieu.
